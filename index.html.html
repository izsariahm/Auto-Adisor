Worn Front Brake Pads Replacement

Understand the Issue & Risk

Brake friction material has worn down to safety squealers. Continuing to drive will wear down to steel backing plates, severely increasing stopping distance and destroying expensive brake rotors.

Difficulty 2 / 5 (Easy-Medium)
Estimated Time 60 - 90 Mins
Est. DIY Savings ~$180

Tools Needed Checklist

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. 1
    Loosen lug nuts & Jack up vehicle: Securely position jack stands on frame rails before removing front wheels.
  2. 2
    Remove Caliper Bolts: Unbolt the top and bottom 14mm guide pin bolts and lift caliper off rotor.
  3. 3
    Compress Piston & Replace Pads: Use C-clamp to push caliper piston back in. Remove old worn pads, insert new pads with brake grease on backings.
